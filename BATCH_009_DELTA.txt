Scope:
 Phase 4A exact physical strap product identity and report-only profile route.

This batch registers the confirmed ULINE S-1655 black PET polyester strap as a
versioned physical product record. Canonical storage is metric: 15.875 mm
nominal width, 0.508 mm nominal thickness, approximately 3336.166 N nominal
break strength, and 868680 mm nominal coil length. Deterministic accessors report
the vendor's 5/8 inch, .020 inch, 750 lbf, and 2,850 foot values.

The record retains the official product-page title, checked date, and explicit
source locator.

The Strap Profile workbench performs validation and reporting only. It does not
render geometry and does not claim measured specimen dimensions, springback,
forming temperature, neutral-axis position, cutting length, or fixture
compensation.
