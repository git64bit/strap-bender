Scope:
 Phase 4C experimental printable radius-calibration coupon.

This batch adds the first physical calibration tooling needed to collect real
radius observations. The coupon is an open inside form: two tangent contact
faces are joined by one circular designed tool radius and extruded vertically
to support the ULINE strap with its width along Z. A separate expanded base
supports the coupon and the lower strap edge.

The Customizer controls designed tool radius, signed bend angle, entry/exit
tangent lengths, form depth/height, base thickness/margin, and bounded tool-
surface tessellation. The tool radius is explicitly NOT a predicted finished
PET radius. No springback law, target-to-tool compensation, forming process,
retention method, heating recommendation, or empirical measurement is added.

Laboratory reference geometry includes 90-degree R1.6 and R5 coupons. These are
experimental CAD tools only and carry no material-behavior authority.
