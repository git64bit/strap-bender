Batch 020 implements:
 - analytical-path diagnostic contract version 1;
 - exact nonlocal primitive-pair diagnostics after every shape front end has
   normalized to the shared finished-inside-edge analytical path;
 - intentional exclusion of sequential primitive neighbors and, for closed
   paths, the first/last continuity pair;
 - exact analytical-bounds prefiltering so distant primitive pairs do not
   require more expensive pair-distance evaluation;
 - exact finite line/line minimum-distance and crossing evaluation;
 - exact finite line/circular-arc intersection and minimum-distance evaluation;
 - exact circular-arc/circular-arc intersection and minimum-distance evaluation;
 - configurable nonnegative near-pass threshold in all four shape workbenches;
 - interaction records retaining analytical primitive indexes, source-command
   indexes, source labels, primitive kinds, exact minimum distance, and
   intersection/near classification;
 - a path-level report containing the total nonlocal pair count, bounds-candidate
   count, intersection count, and near-pass count;
 - non-rejecting behavior: detected analytical intersections are reported but do
   not yet change the authored shape or automatically reject it;
 - a direct-open contract covering line/line crossing and clearance, line/arc
   tangency and clearance, arc/arc crossing, nonadjacent-pair selection,
   provenance, report validation, and classification;
 - public-foundation version/constructor/accessor coverage.

Important Batch 020 boundary:
 The near threshold is a geometric diagnostic on the finished-inside-edge
 analytical reference. It is NOT a physical strap-width, strap-thickness,
 springback, or fixture-clearance requirement. Existing fixture-clearance
 validation remains the physical tooling collision policy.

This batch deliberately does NOT add:
 - a rule that automatically rejects every analytical self-intersection;
 - a crossing fixture topology;
 - empirical target-to-tool radius compensation;
 - neutral-axis or developed/cut-length correction;
 - closure overlap, trimming, welding, fastening, or joining allowances;
 - strap-solid rendering;
 - manufacturing/export manifests;
 - Catalog promotion.
