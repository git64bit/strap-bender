Scope:
 Phase 2A named vertex-polygon front end.

 Implements closed ordered-vertex source records with a common radius or one
 explicit desired finished inside radius per corner. Derives polygon orientation,
 signed turns, orientation-independent convex/concave classification, circular
 tangent setbacks, tangent entry/exit points, and retained straight lengths.
 Feasible polygons normalize to the existing arbitrary-length bend-program
 record and therefore use the same exact analytical path, sampling, reporting,
 and diagnostic rendering pipeline.

Authority and provenance:
 Sharp ordered polygon vertices and requested corner radii remain the authoring
 source. Generated corner and edge records are derived execution data. Each
 derived bend records its source vertex and normalized bend-command index; each
 retained straight records its source edge and normalized straight-command index.
 Exact analytical line and circular-arc primitives remain authoritative after
 normalization.

Not included:
 No regular triangle/square/pentagon generator.
 No periodic or formula-based radius schedule grammar.
 No polygon self-intersection or near-intersection analysis.
 No strap solid, material calibration, cut length, or fixture geometry.
