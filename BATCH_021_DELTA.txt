 - a source cutting-policy record separated from the derived cutting plan;
 - exact strap-material lookup for nominal thickness provenance;
 - nominal_mid_thickness development mode using exactly 0.5 of vendor nominal
   thickness outward from the finished inside edge;
 - custom_fraction development mode with an explicit fraction from 0 through 1;
 - exact decomposition of the analytical path into straight and inside-edge arc
   lengths;
 - nominal developed arc length from each exact circular analytical primitive;
 - nominal developed strap length independent of display sampling;
 - independent explicit start and end allowances;
 - explicit closure modes none, butt, and overlap;
 - explicit overlap and joining allowances with zero defaults;
 - open-path validation requiring no closure overlap or joining allowance;
 - closed overlap validation requiring a positive explicit overlap;
 - an explicit closure-unassigned status for closed geometry with no selected
   physical seam policy;
 - nominal cut length = nominal developed length + explicit allowances;
 - millimetre and foot reporting for developed and cut lengths;
 - full bend-by-bend development-radius/arc reporting;
 - nominal cut-plan controls in Bend Program, Vertex Polygon, Regular Polygon,
   and Wave Pattern workbenches;
 - material routing for cut planning independent of fixture rendering;
 - a direct-open contract covering open and closed cutting policies;
 - public-foundation version, constructor, accessor, planning, and validation
   coverage.

Important Batch 021 boundary:
 The default 0.5 thickness fraction is a geometric nominal assumption only. It
 is NOT a measured PET neutral-axis location. The custom fraction is an explicit
 user assumption, not a fitted material parameter. Every generated cut plan is
 therefore marked nominal_uncalibrated or
 nominal_uncalibrated_closure_unassigned.

No hidden cutting allowance exists. Start allowance, end allowance, overlap,
 and joining allowance each default to zero and are reported independently.

This batch deliberately does NOT add:
 - empirical target-to-tool radius compensation;
 - calibrated neutral-axis or K-factor fitting;
 - empirical cut-length correction;
 - a prescribed welding, fastening, interlocking, or trimming process;
 - automatic overlap or joining values;
 - strap-solid rendering;
 - manufacturing/export manifests;
 - Catalog promotion.
