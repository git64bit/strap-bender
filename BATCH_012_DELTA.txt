Scope:
 Phase 4D guarded coupon-linked radius-observation entry.

This batch adds the evidence-entry bridge between the printable radius coupons
from Batch 011 and the radius-observation record from Batch 010. The new Radius
Observation workbench selects one exact Laboratory coupon and defaults to a
DRAFT state. While Observation ready is false, the coupon is validated and
reported but no radius-observation evidence record is constructed.

When the operator explicitly enables Observation ready, specimen dimensions,
forming conditions, finished-radius measurement, method, date, uncertainty,
and notes are used to construct one transient radius observation. Strap material,
signed bend angle, and designed tool radius are derived from the selected coupon,
not retyped by the operator.

A new calibration-trial record preserves the exact coupon name together with the
embedded observation. Trial validation resolves that coupon by exact name and
requires the observation material, signed bend angle, and tool radius to match.
This is provenance and consistency checking only. It is not a springback model,
forming recommendation, target-to-tool mapping, or certification that entered
values are physical measurements.

No real PET observation is registered by this batch. All ready-state contract
values are explicitly synthetic and future-dated.
