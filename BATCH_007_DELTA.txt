Scope:
 Phase 2C compact numeric value schedules and polygon radius routing.

 Implements native, versioned numeric value-schedule records with four kinds:
 constant, explicit, periodic, and every_nth. Schedule resolution is independent
 of polygon geometry and accepts an arbitrary consumer count. The every_nth rule
 uses human-facing one-based source positions, so interval 3 with first position
 3 selects positions 3, 6, 9, and so on.

 Both vertex-polygon and regular-polygon sources may now retain a compact radius
 schedule. Validation resolves exactly one finite positive radius per source
 vertex before tangent setbacks are calculated. Regular-polygon compilation
 writes the resolved explicit radii into its generated vertex-polygon record,
 preserving the existing bend-program and analytical-path contracts.

 The Regular Polygon Customizer now offers constant and every_nth radius modes.
 Its default diagnostic example is a nine-sided shape using 1.6 mm radii except
 for 5 mm radii at source positions 3, 6, and 9. Full explicit and periodic lists
 remain available in Laboratory records. A named Laboratory nonagon preserves the
 same every-third rule without establishing a fixed bend count.

Authority and provenance:
 The compact schedule is authoritative authoring intent. Its resolved list is
 derived for a known consumer count and must not be maintained as a second source
 of truth. For polygons, schedule positions refer to ordered source vertices.
 Changing start_vertex_index may change normalized command traversal but does not
 renumber source schedule positions or V# provenance.

Not included:
 No arbitrary formula or callback schedule grammar.
 No named pattern block, pattern instance, or long-wave compiler.
 No polygon or analytical-path self-intersection diagnostic.
 No strap solid, material calibration, neutral-axis cut length, or fixture.
