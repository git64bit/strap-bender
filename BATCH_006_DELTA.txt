Scope:
 Phase 2B regular-polygon front end.

 Implements native regular-polygon source and compilation records. A regular
 source defines side count, one governing sharp dimension, center, orientation,
 start vertex, and common or explicit desired finished inside radii. Supported
 governing dimensions are sharp side length, sharp circumradius, and sharp
 apothem. The compiler resolves all three dimensions, generates ordered
 counter-clockwise sharp vertices, and creates a standard vertex-polygon record.
 That record then uses the accepted rounded-polygon, bend-program, analytical,
 sampling, reporting, and diagnostic-rendering pipeline.

 The executable Regular Polygon workbench exposes scalar Customizer controls for
 side count, governing dimension, common corner radius, first-vertex angle, and
 center. It does not establish a fixed internal side or bend count. Named
 Laboratory examples cover a triangle, square, and mixed-radius pentagon.

Authority and provenance:
 The regular-polygon source record remains authoritative authoring intent. Its
 governing sharp dimension is not rewritten by corner rounding. Resolved
 dimensions, generated sharp vertices, and the generated vertex-polygon record
 are derived compilation data. After vertex generation, existing E# and V#
 labels preserve source edge and vertex identities through normalized commands
 and exact analytical primitives.

Not included:
 No periodic, formula-based, or index-derived radius schedule grammar.
 No polygon self-intersection or near-intersection diagnostic.
 No pattern or long-wave compiler.
 No strap solid, material calibration, neutral-axis cut length, or fixture.
