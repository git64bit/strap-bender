Scope:
 Phase 4B physical radius-observation evidence contract.

This batch adds the record boundary needed to preserve one real bend observation
without inventing or fitting PET behavior. An observation records the referenced
strap product, specimen identity and measured dimensions, bend angle, tool inside
radius, forming method and temperature, dwell, cooling restraint, post-release
rest time, measured finished inside radius, measurement method/date, stated
measurement uncertainty, and notes.

Direct finished-minus-tool radius delta and dimensionless radius ratios are derived
from one valid observation. They are descriptive measurements only. This batch
does not fit a target-to-tool compensation law, define a neutral-axis correction,
calculate cutting length, or create calibration-coupon/production fixture geometry.

No physical observation is registered by this batch. Test observations are
explicitly synthetic and must not be treated as PET forming recommendations.
