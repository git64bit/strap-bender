Scope:
 Phase 2 bounded follow-up: sharp-source polygon self-intersection diagnostics.

This batch is intentionally independent of the blocked physical-calibration path.
No PET springback values, target-to-tool mapping, neutral-axis model, cut-length
correction, or production fixture topology are introduced.

The diagnostic compares only nonadjacent edges of the ordered sharp source
polygon. Shared endpoints of adjacent edges are expected and are ignored.
Nonadjacent proper crossings, touches, and collinear contacts are reported as
stable source-edge index pairs.

This batch deliberately does NOT reject a polygon merely because the diagnostic
finds an intersection. The project still has an open fixture-policy decision
about whether any self-crossing analytical target can ever be supported.
Analytical line/arc self-intersection and near-intersection analysis remain later
work.
