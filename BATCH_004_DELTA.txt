Scope:
 Phase 1C sampled display path and diagnostic rendering.
 Derives a chordal display polyline from the exact finished-inside-edge
 analytical line/arc path. Sampling is constrained independently by requested
 maximum chord error and maximum angular step. Adds sampled-path records,
 accessors, validation, reporting, point-count protection, and a thin 3D
 diagnostic preview with analytical tangent-point markers.

Authority boundary:
 Exact analytical lines and circular arcs remain authoritative. Sampling may
 change display point count and approximate chordal length, but may not alter
 analytical length, exact bounds, stations, closure, or future fixture datums.
 The diagnostic render is not a strap solid, neutral-axis model, mandrel, or
 production fixture.

Not included:
 No polygon or regular-polygon front end.
 No wave-pattern compiler or schedule grammar.
 No strap specification, neutral-axis model, or cut-length calculation.
 No forming compensation or radius calibration.
 No production fixture geometry.
 No self-intersection or near-intersection analysis.
