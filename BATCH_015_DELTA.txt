Scope:
 Phase 5 bounded start: first production-shape fixture software family.

Development priority:
 Pending coupon tests do not block application development. This batch advances
 fixture planning and printable geometry while preserving the evidence boundary.
 Until physical calibration exists, the tool-radius policy is explicitly
 nominal/uncompensated and no relaxed-PET result is claimed.

Selected first family:
 - full-form rectangular base for shapes that fit one print envelope;
 - one full cylindrical inside-form post per exact analytical arc;
 - target and tool centers/tangent datums remain separate in the derived record;
 - nominal_target mode makes target/tool radius, center, and tangent datums equal;
 - exact source-command index, label, global station interval, turn, and datums
   remain traceable;
 - base bounds cover the exact analytical path and complete post circles plus
   configurable margin;
 - configured print-envelope width/depth are asserting limits;
 - post height must be at least the nominal strap width;
 - post circular tessellation obeys chord-error and angular-step limits;
 - fixture status is experimental_uncompensated;
 - retention_mode is none: the first family is open-top and has no integral
   clamp, follower, or clearance channel.

This batch deliberately does NOT add:
 - empirical target-to-tool radius compensation;
 - springback or forming-process assumptions;
 - neutral-axis or cut-length correction;
 - strap solid/clearance geometry;
 - post/post or post/nonlocal-path interference detection;
 - long-form segmentation, joints, component manifests, or assembly datums;
 - Catalog promotion.
