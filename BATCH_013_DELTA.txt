Scope:
 Phase 4E persistent calibration-evidence registry boundary.

This batch creates the explicit persistence point for reviewed physical
coupon-linked calibration trials. Batch 012 can construct one transient ready
trial from Customizer input; Batch 013 deliberately does not promote that
transient record automatically. Persistent evidence enters the repository only
through an explicit registry change after physical values and provenance have
been supplied and reviewed.

The new Laboratory calibration-trial registry is delivered empty. No PET
springback value, forming recommendation, target-to-tool mapping, neutral-axis
correction, cutting-length correction, or production fixture geometry is
invented by this batch.

The new Calibration Evidence workbench is report-only. It validates that
persistent trial names are unique, revalidates each stored trial against the
active strap-material and exact source-coupon registries, reports the physical
trial count, and explicitly states that compensation fitting remains blocked
while the registry is empty.

Synthetic records used by the new contract test remain local to that test and
are never registered as physical evidence.
